g-i: Groww's own MTF net book (IR page; Rs Cr) and the calibration factor k per quarter. A reported quarter's daily revenue = k x own book x 14.95% / FY trading days, with k = disclosed MTF revenue / (sum of own book x 14.95% / FY days), so the quarter total is again the disclosed revenue (g-f's calibration, with the own book as the daily shape). The open quarter has no disclosed revenue: k = 1, i.e. the formula as stated.

Book basis of the MTF days: interpolated 496, latest 13. Rule: an archived level for the day, else (closed quarter) linear interpolation in calendar days between the two quarter-end levels, else (open quarter) the latest level held flat. g-i also covers every calendar equity day (509 to the as-of date), so it fills the days the industry-book series lacks (2026-04-08, 2026-10-21).
