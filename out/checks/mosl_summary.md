## Where a switch does something different from the spec (or the bridge presents it differently)

1. **"MOSL broking = consolidated net brokerage" is not quite what the model sums.** The data book's net brokerage total (Q2 FY27 277.41 Cr, Consol row 56) = cash + F&O + commodity + currency. The model's Broking is cash + F&O + commodity only (Q2 FY27 276.64 Cr); currency net brokerage (Q2 FY27 0.773 Cr, 0.28% of the total; Q1 FY27 1.095 Cr, 0.40%) is in no panel. Effect: Broking and Consolidated ADR are low by about 0.012 Cr per day in Q2 FY27 (0.018 in Q1 FY27). The methodology and spec do not mention currency; either add it or label the panel "cash + F&O + commodity".
2. **m-d bridge, Q2 FY27 "YoY" table.** The "Workbook YoY (vs Q4 FY26)" column keeps the 1 Feb 2026 budget session in Q4 FY26 (the workbook's convention), while the Q4 FY26 column printed beside it excludes it. The printed columns therefore do not reproduce the workbook column (Consolidated 16.2% shown, 15.9% from the printed columns; commodities -34.3% vs -35.2%). I reproduced the 16.2 / -4.2 / +43.8 / -12.8 / +19.7 / -34.3 only with the session kept. The live YoY column (vs Q2 FY26) agrees.
3. **After m-e the October segments end on different days**: F&O and cash 13 days to 21 Oct, commodities 14 (the MCX-only 20 Oct included), MTF 12 days to 19 Oct (industry book ends there). Oct 2026 Consolidated sums ADRs over different day sets (Methodology issue 10; no switch in m-a ... m-f changes it).
4. **m-a / m-c / m-d intermediate states mix bases for FY27** (F&O to 21 Oct, cash and commodities to 30 Sep, MTF to 30 Sep, until m-e adds October). Not an end-state issue; the m-c bridge says so.
5. **Q1 / Q2 FY25 MTF**: the reported book and typed yield of these quarters are not used (the daily industry book starts 1 Oct 2024, so there are no MTF rows; those periods are flagged insufficient), so "every reported quarter calibrated" applies to MTF from Q3 FY25.
6. m-d leaves the calibration spreading each quarter over every row, special sessions included; only the averages leave them out. A quarter's ADR x trading days is therefore a little below its data-book net where a special session sits in it (Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26). Consistent with the spec as written.
