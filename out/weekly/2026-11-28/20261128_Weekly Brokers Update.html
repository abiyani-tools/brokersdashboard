Brokers Update: Angel One · Groww · Motilal Oswal

Revenue run-rate by segment, F&O orders, MTF, PAT run-rate and valuation

Data to the close of 27 Nov 2026 · ADR = average daily revenue, ₹ Cr per trading day · broker figures are the dashboard's estimates until Q3 FY27 is reported

Key takeaways

  • Best quarter so far for all three. Q3 FY27 ADR to 27 Nov: Groww ₹23.2 Cr (+25% QoQ, +43% YoY), Angel One ₹20.1 Cr (+22%, +24%), Motilal Oswal ₹9.9 Cr (+11%, +22%). Each is the highest quarter in the dashboard's series.
  • F&O is doing the work. It accounts for 77% of Angel's QoQ increase and 76% of Groww's. F&O ADR is up about 33% at all three brokers.
  • Brokers capture less of the volatility than NSE. F&O ADR is up about 33%, in line with F&O orders/day (Angel 3.93 → 5.25 Mn). NSE's ADR is up 47% because its fees also rise with option premium. Higher VIX helps brokers mainly through order counts.
  • Flat week. Consolidated ADR for the last 5 sessions vs the 5 before: Angel −2.2%, Groww −0.5%, MOSL −1.2%. F&O was up 10–12%, but commodity fell 42% because a one-day spike on 17 Nov dropped out of the window.
  • The MTF book has stopped growing. NSE's book was ₹1,47,019 Cr on 26 Nov, 2.7% below its 30 Sep peak (₹1,51,134 Cr). MTF ADR is up only 2–5% QoQ. Groww's share of the exchanges' book has grown more than 4x in seven quarters (0.63% → 2.74%).
  • Angel is now close to its target. It rose 8.0% this week and 37% since 30 Sep, to ₹376. On our 40% ratio / 60% ADR blend the Base target is ₹388 (+3%). Groww (₹222) has more room: Base ₹244 (+10%).
